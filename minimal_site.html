The Art of Doing Less

In a world obsessed with productivity, the most radical act is to pause. We often confuse movement with progress, yet the stillest waters run the deepest.

When we clear away the clutter of our schedules, we find the space required for meaningful creation. This is not about laziness; it is about essentialism.

"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." — Leonardo da Vinci

Focus on the one thing that matters today. Let the rest fall away. The noise will always be there, but your attention is a finite resource. Protect it fiercely.
